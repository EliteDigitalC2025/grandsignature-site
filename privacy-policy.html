Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Overview

GrandSignature Properties respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website or contact us about real estate opportunities.

This policy describes the types of information that may be processed through our website, why that information is used and how you can contact us with privacy-related questions.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number.
  • Preferred property location, type, budget and other requirements you choose to share.
  • Messages submitted through our contact form, email or WhatsApp.
  • Basic technical information processed by website hosting or service providers, such as IP addresses and request logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests for property information.
  • Discuss property preferences and viewing arrangements.
  • Communicate about enquiries you have initiated.
  • Maintain necessary business correspondence and records.
  • Support website operation, security and troubleshooting.

Marketing communications, where offered, are handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements. You may request to stop receiving marketing messages.

4. Service Providers and Information Sharing

We use third-party services to support website functionality and communication. Our contact form is configured to submit enquiries through Formspree, which processes the information entered into the form.

Communications sent through WhatsApp or email are also processed through the relevant service providers.

Information may be shared with appropriate service providers, professional advisers or relevant parties when necessary to respond to a requested service or comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Cookies and External Services

Website delivery may involve technical logs and data storage used by hosting and third-party providers. Our pages may load external resources, including property photographs and embedded maps, which can result in information being transmitted to those providers.

You can manage cookies and website data through your browser settings. Disabling certain browser features may affect the functionality of some services.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain relevant business records, fulfil the purposes for which it was collected and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required is handled in accordance with applicable retention and deletion requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate data, deletion in appropriate circumstances, or restriction or objection to certain processing.

You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

8. Data Security

We take the responsible handling of personal information seriously and seek to use appropriate safeguards for the services we operate.

No method of online transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Visitors are encouraged to contact us through our published communication channels when sharing information about property enquiries.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, website functionality or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be available on this page with its corresponding update date.
