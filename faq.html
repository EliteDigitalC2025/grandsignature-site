Buying & Due Diligence

We can discuss property documentation questions and help identify matters requiring professional review. Buyers should engage an independent qualified Nigerian property lawyer and appropriate tax adviser to verify title, approvals, transaction documents and applicable obligations.

Important checks include the seller's authority, title documents, survey details, planning approvals, physical condition, access, service charges and total transaction costs. Independent legal and technical due diligence is recommended before payment.
